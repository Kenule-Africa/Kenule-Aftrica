# Strict Rule: No Em Dashes

## Prohibition on Em Dashes
- Never use em dashes (`—`, `\u2014`, `&mdash;`, `&#8212;`) or en dashes (`–`, `\u2013`, `&ndash;`, `&#8211;`) in any copy, headings, descriptions, labels, or UI text across this entire website and codebase.
- Use clean commas (`,`), standard hyphens (`-`), colons (`:`), or rewrite the sentence naturally instead.
- This rule applies unconditionally to all present and future changes.
